Slide 2 / 6 · The Story of AlphaNexus CashTokens Sentinel

Why Manual 64-Hex Category Checks Fail Under Stress

While reading the official homepage of ParyonUSD (PUSD) — the stablecoin sponsor of BCH BLAZE 2026 — it hit us: ParyonUSD explicitly warns every user on its front page: "Always confirm this category ID (2469acc5afa4b10cb5b5c04afb89c3a3ffd61c5da9c01e26d00951cae2a02544) matches the token in your wallet or on a DEX before sending or trading."

On Bitcoin Cash CashTokens, a token's true identity is its 32-byte genesis category ID — anyone can mint a brand-new category with the exact same ticker (PUSD, MUSD, FURU) or homograph (PU5D). Asking retail users or autonomous trading bots to eyeball 64 hex characters before every swap is a recipe for costly mistakes. So we built an automated, fail-closed CashTokens Sentinel!

Slide 3 / 6 · Benefits of AlphaNexus CashTokens Sentinel

5 Deterministic Rules — Zero LLM Hallucinations

  • Instant BCMR v2 Verification: Cross-checks candidate tokens against trusted Bitcoin Cash Metadata Registries (ParyonUSD + OpenTokenRegistry otr.cash).
  • Rule R1 (32-Byte Hex Integrity): Rejects malformed or non-standard Category IDs immediately (BLOCK).
  • Rule R2 (Ticker & Homograph Anti-Impersonation): Normalizes confusable characters (0/O, 1/l, 5/S, $/S). Any token claiming PUSD or PU5D with a different category ID is intercepted pre-trade (BLOCK).
  • Rule R3 (Fail-Closed Unknown Category Gate): Unregistered categories trigger FAIL_CLOSED_REVIEW — autonomous bots are forbidden from auto-buying until human approval.
  • Rules R4 & R5 (Live Minting NFT & Whale Concentration): Detects active minting capability NFTs (supply inflation risk) and >60% / >90% top-10 holder concentration from UTXO snapshots.
